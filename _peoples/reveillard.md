**[Arthur Réveillard](https://www.timc.fr/Arthur-REVEILLARD)** is a PhD student supervised by [Fabien Pierrel](https://www.timc.fr/en/fabien-pierrel) and [Ahcène Boumendjel](https://lrb.univ-grenoble-alpes.fr/ahcene-boumendjel), with the contribution of [Sophie Abby](https://sophieabby.github.io/). He works on the Identification of isoprenoid quinones from environmental lipidomes by developping a molecular networking approach.
